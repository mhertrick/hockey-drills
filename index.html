3-zone 3v2 Breakout & Attack

Offense (O2, O3) Offense D (O1) Defense (X) Coach (C) Puck Bumper

Drill Setup

The ice is dynamically divided to support two simultaneous 3v2 drills. An imaginary boundary (shown as an orange dashed line in the animation) runs straight down the center of the neutral zone. When this line hits either blue line, it angles directly to the near post of the net. To ensure safety and prevent collisions around the net, a bumper pad is placed near the goal along this imaginary dividing line, physically separating the offensive and defensive plays. Player paths are carefully spaced to ensure full-speed execution without interfering with waiting lines or coaches.

  • Players per Unit: 5 Players (3 Offensive players as O's, 2 Defending Defensemen as X's). The 3 O's consist of an offensive defenseman (O1, purple), a wing (O2), and a center (O3).
  • Starting Position: The active unit lines up at the center red line. The next group waits in line inside the center faceoff circle, right along the dividing boundary line, ready to step up.
  • Coaches: Coach 1 (C1) is stationed safely below the goal line to apply light pressure on the puck retrieval. Coach 2 (C2) stands in the neutral zone near the waiting players. Coach 3 (C3) stands in a safe observing position at the exact intersection of the offensive blue line and the imaginary dividing line.

Execution Sequence

  1. Dump In: On Coach 2's whistle, the defending defenseman (X1) dumps the puck from the center red line deep into the defensive corner.
  2. Retrieval & Support:
    • O1 (Offensive Defenseman): Pivots and skates back aggressively to retrieve the puck, absorbing low pressure from Coach 1 (C1) below the goal line.
    • O2 (Wing): Skates down to the hash marks on the boards to provide an outlet passing lane.
    • O3 (Center): Enters the zone, looping low near the faceoff dot to support the play and build speed up the ice.
  3. The Breakout: O1 passes to O2 along the boards. O2 one-touch passes to O3 who is accelerating out of the zone. O1 then follows behind O2 and O3 as they leave the defensive zone.
  4. Defensive Gap: As the offense retrieves the puck, X1 and X2 follow the play into the zone to establish a tight defensive gap, pivoting to skate backward as the breakout develops.
  5. Neutral Zone & Attack: Beyond the initial breakout pattern, passing in the neutral zone and the method of attack are not strictly prescribed. The forwards (O2 and O3) should read the tight defensive gap, adapt, and move the puck creatively as the play develops to execute the 3v2 attack.
  6. Offensive Support (O1): O1 moves closely behind O2 and O3 as part of a tight unit up the ice, but stays high in the offensive zone near the blue line to protect the zone and keep pucks in.
  7. Reset: The drill ends when a goal is scored, the goalie freezes the puck, or the defense successfully clears it. All 5 players must clear the zone, and the next unit waiting at center ice begins.

Constraints & Rules

  • Time Limit: The offense has exactly 10 seconds to generate a shot on goal after crossing the offensive blue line.
  • Defensive Responsibility: Defensemen must maintain tight gaps, stick-on-puck positioning, and attempt to force the attackers to the outside.
  • Turnovers: If the defense (X1/X2) disrupts the play or steals the puck, they must complete one successful pass to a coach or clear the puck past the red line to end the rep.
  • Offsides: Strictly enforced at the attacking blue line.

Modifications & Progressions

  • Opposite Side Breakout: To practice breaking out from the opposite boards, simply have the teams execute the drill in the reverse direction. They stay in their same lane (top or bottom) but dump the puck into the opposite end zone, mirroring the entire flow and forcing them to break out from the other side. Use the orange "Switch to Reverse" button below the animation to visualize this.
  • Regroup Variation: Instead of attacking immediately, the offense must pass back to Coach 2 in the neutral zone for a quick regroup before re-attacking the 3v2.
  • Continuous Flow: To increase conditioning and pace, the next group of 5 dumps their puck the exact moment the active group crosses the attacking blue line.
