CBHL 10U Continuous 3v2 Breakout & Attack

Offense (O) Defense (X) Puck

Drill Setup

The ice is dynamically divided to support two simultaneous 3v2 drills. An imaginary boundary (shown as an orange dashed line in the animation) runs straight down the center of the neutral zone. When this line hits either blue line, it angles directly to the near post of the net. This split guarantees that Team A (Top Lane) has the entire top corner for their breakout and access to the top half of the far net for their attack, while Team B (Bottom Lane) operates on the opposite angles.

  • Players per Unit: 5 Players (3 Offensive players as O's, 2 Defending Defensemen as X's). The 3 O's consist of an offensive defenseman (O1), a wing (O2), and a center (O3).
  • Starting Position: Both teams line up at the center red line on their respective side of the boundary, facing their defensive zone.

Execution Sequence

  1. Dump In: On the whistle, the defending defenseman (X1) dumps the puck from the center red line deep into the defensive corner.
  2. Retrieval & Support:
    • O1 (Offensive Defenseman): Pivots and skates back aggressively to retrieve the puck.
    • O2 (Wing): Skates down to the hash marks on the boards to provide an outlet passing lane.
    • O3 (Center): Enters the zone, looping low near the faceoff dot to support the play and build speed up the ice.
  3. The Breakout: O1 passes to O2 along the boards. O2 can either carry or one-touch pass to O3 who is accelerating out of the zone. O1 then joins O2 and O3 in the rush up the ice.
  4. Defensive Gap: As the offense retrieves the puck, X1 and X2 follow the play into the zone to establish a tight defensive gap, pivoting to skate backward as the breakout develops.
  5. Neutral Zone & Attack: The offensive trio (O1, O2, O3) executes a 3-on-2 rush through the neutral zone against X1 and X2, crossing the offensive blue line to generate a scoring chance.
  6. Reset: The drill ends when a goal is scored, the goalie freezes the puck, or the defense successfully clears it. All 5 players must clear the zone to allow the next unit to begin.

Constraints & Rules

  • Time Limit: The offense has exactly 10 seconds to generate a shot on goal after crossing the offensive blue line.
  • Defensive Responsibility: Defensemen must maintain tight gaps, stick-on-puck positioning, and attempt to force the attackers to the outside.
  • Turnovers: If the defense (X1/X2) disrupts the play or steals the puck, they must complete one successful pass to a coach or clear the puck past the red line to end the rep.
  • Offsides: Strictly enforced at the attacking blue line.

Modifications & Progressions

  • Regroup Variation: Instead of attacking immediately, the offense must pass back to a coach positioned in the neutral zone for a quick regroup before re-attacking the 3v2.
  • Pressure Release: A coach applies light physical pressure (a bump or stick check) to O1 during the puck retrieval, forcing them to protect the puck before making the breakout pass.
  • Continuous Flow: To increase conditioning and pace, the next group of 5 dumps their puck the exact moment the active group crosses the attacking blue line.
